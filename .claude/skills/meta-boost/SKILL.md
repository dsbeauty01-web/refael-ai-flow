# Boosting a Refael.ai post

Boost = the quick "Boost post" screen on the Page. Use it for a first cheap test. Move to Ads Manager (see the `ads` skill) once something wins.

## Settings (Israel widget offer, Hebrew reel)

| Field | Set to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Messages (WhatsApp) or "More messages" | We close on WhatsApp |
| Message destination | WhatsApp number of the Refael.ai business (the Page, not the personal/dance account) | Leads must land in the business inbox |
| Special Ad Category | **None** | Meta suggests "Employment" because of the page category. It is wrong for us: we sell a service, not jobs. Picking it locks targeting and adds review. |
| Location | **Israel** (never leave the default Thailand: the default comes from where the phone is) | Hebrew ad, Israeli buyers, shekel offer |
| Age | 28 to 60 | Business owners |
| Advantage+ audience | On | Broad works best on Meta in 2026; the creative does the targeting |
| Placements | Facebook + Instagram, automatic | Reels placements are where the 9:16 video belongs |
| Budget | ~₪50/day (≈ ฿500) per reel | Enough data for a 3 to 4 day read |
| Duration | **Set an end date: 4 days.** Do not run "continuously" | Avoid a forgotten spend |
| Payment | Refael confirms the card himself | Claude never enters or confirms payment |

Currency: if the ad account is in THB, ₪50 ≈ ฿480 to ฿500 per day (check the rate).

## Read after 4 days

- Hook rate (3-second views / impressions) at least 25%: keep. Under 20%: kill, boost the next reel (23:04, then night, then line).
- Cost per WhatsApp conversation: the real metric. Write each number down.
- No conversations at all after ₪200: the reel gets attention but not intent. Change the reel or the offer, not the budget.

## Rules

- One boost at a time per concept; never boost 4 reels at once at ₪50 each before one has data.
- Ads must follow Meta's personal attributes policy: no "Are you a clinic owner?". Describe the business, not the person.
- Toggle Meta's "AI info" label when the ad contains an AI character.
- Never claim a face raises sales; the research cards in the reel are small studies and must stay labelled.
