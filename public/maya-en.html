Questions that come up

Is it really live, or a recorded video?

Live. She reads the comments during the stream and answers in real time, usually within about 5 seconds. In our last live she answered 10 out of 10 comments, each by the commenter's name.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. She can host the live on her own, or work alongside you: you show the product, she answers the chat.

What if someone asks her something she doesn't know?

She doesn't make things up. She answers only from what you prepared (products, prices, shipping, returns), and anything beyond that she hands to you.

Can I get a different character, or my own voice?

Yes. Maya and Nova are examples. We can build a character around your brand, and clone your own voice (with your permission).

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of products and lives. Send me a product photo on WhatsApp, see it live, and I'll tell you exactly.
