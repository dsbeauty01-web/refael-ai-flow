Last updated: 21 October 2026 · refael-ai-flow.lovable.app · עברית

Refael.ai is operated by Refael Sela, an Israeli sole proprietor (Osek Patur). This policy explains what information the website and the Refael.ai Facebook Page app collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

Who controls your data

Data controller: Refael Sela (Sole proprietor, Osek Patur) · dsbeauty01@gmail.com · +972-53-321-7125.

What we collect on the website

Information you submit voluntarily through the contact form or WhatsApp: name, phone number, business name, and any product photo you choose to send us. Technical information collected automatically while browsing (IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed) is used only to operate and secure the site. Your language preference is stored in your own browser and is not sent to us.

Facebook Page comments and Messenger

Our Facebook Page posts invite people to comment a keyword (for example "מיה") to receive a demo link. When you comment that keyword on one of our Page's posts, our app reads that comment and sends you one private reply in Messenger with the link you asked for, plus a short public reply under your comment. To do this we process your public Facebook name, your Facebook user ID as provided to our Page, the comment text and its ID.

We message only people who commented on our own Page's posts, only once per comment, and only within 7 days of the comment. We never send unsolicited messages, never sell or share this data, and never use it for advertising. We keep your name, ID and comment for up to 12 months so we do not message you twice and can continue the conversation you started; then it is deleted.

Voice conversations with an avatar

Live avatar demos require your explicit microphone permission, which you can revoke at any time. Audio is processed in real time only to conduct the conversation; we do not record or store it and never use it to train models.

How the information is used

To answer your request, send the demo or clip you asked for, give a quote and schedule a pilot. We do not sell your information or pass it to third parties for marketing.

Service providers

The site is cloud-hosted, and messages pass through automation and AI services (for example Meta, n8n, ElevenLabs, fal.ai) that process data only on our behalf. Some are located outside Israel, so data may be stored on servers abroad.

Data deletion instructions

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you, including data received through Facebook or Messenger:

  1. Send a Messenger message to the Refael.ai Page saying "delete my data", or email dsbeauty01@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name on your Facebook profile.
  2. We delete your records within 30 days and confirm by the same channel.

You can also remove the app's access from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Remove.

Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981 (and the GDPR where it applies), you may review, correct or delete the information held about you. Write to dsbeauty01@gmail.com.

Cookies

No advertising or tracking cookies. Local storage only remembers your language.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
